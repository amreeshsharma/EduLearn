1. Introduction

Welcome to EduLearn ("we", "our", or "us"). We recognize that your privacy is of the utmost importance. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, store, utilize, and protect your personal information when you access our educational website, enroll in our courses, or interact with our learning management system.

By accessing or creating an account on EduLearn, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide and enhance our educational services to you:

  • Personal Identification Data: Full Name, email address, phone number, and account credentials entered during registration.
  • Course Progress Data: Completed lessons, quiz scores, project submissions, and certificate records.
  • Payment Transaction Records: Order IDs and billing history processed through encrypted payment gateways (we do not store credit card CVV or passwords).
  • Technical Logs: IP address, browser type, operating system, and page interaction timestamps.

3. How We Use Your Information

The collected information is utilized strictly for educational delivery and platform operations:

  • To authenticate your account and personalize your student dashboard.
  • To track your course milestones and generate verifiable completion certificates.
  • To send critical notifications, enrollment confirmations, and instructor announcements.
  • To diagnose technical bugs, prevent fraud, and optimize page load speeds.
Our Guarantee: EduLearn never sells, rents, or monetizes your personal contact information with third-party advertisers.

4. Data Protection & Security

We employ industry-standard TLS 1.3 encryption for all data transmitted across the web. Passwords are cryptographically hashed using salted SHA-256 algorithms. Our databases are secured behind enterprise firewalls with automated daily backups.

5. Cookies & Local Storage

EduLearn uses essential session cookies to keep you signed in securely and retain your course playback position. You can choose to disable non-essential cookies via your browser settings, though some interactive features may function with reduced convenience.

6. Third-Party Service Providers

We partner with accredited third-party providers for specific platform functions, including cloud server hosting (AWS/DigitalOcean), high-speed video CDNs, and PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways. All partners adhere to strict confidentiality and privacy agreements.

7. Your Privacy Rights

As an EduLearn student, you hold full control over your personal data:

  • Access & Export: Request a complete copy of your profile and learning records.
  • Correction: Update outdated profile details directly from your account settings.
  • Right to Erasure: Request permanent deletion of your account and personal records at any time.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect technological advances or legal requirements. Any significant revisions will be highlighted on our homepage and notified to your registered email address.
