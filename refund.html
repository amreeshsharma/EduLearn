Refund Policy

We want you to learn with absolute confidence. Enjoy our hassle-free 30-Day Money-Back Guarantee on all individual course enrollments.

100% 30-Day Money-Back Guarantee

If you are not completely satisfied with your course quality or curriculum depth within the first 30 days of purchase, you can request a full refund—no tricky questions asked.

1. Refund Eligibility Criteria

To qualify for a 100% refund under our standard guarantee:

  • The refund request must be submitted within 30 calendar days of the original purchase date.
  • The student has viewed or completed less than 50% of the total course video content.
  • A verified Certificate of Completion has not yet been generated or downloaded.

2. Step-by-Step Refund Process

Getting a refund on EduLearn is simple, transparent, and swift:

1

Submit Request

Email support or fill the contact form with your Order ID.

2

Ticket Review

Our support team verifies enrollment timestamp and progress.

3

Approval

Refund is approved and transaction initiation email is sent.

4

Credit Back

Funds credited back directly to your original payment method.

3. Non-Refundable Situations

Refunds will not be issued under the following circumstances:

  • Requests submitted after the 30-day window has expired.
  • Accounts flagged for academic dishonesty, terms of service violations, or video scraping.
  • If all course downloadable resources and capstone source codes have already been exported.

4. Processing Time & Method

Once approved, refunds are processed immediately on our end. Depending on your bank, UPI provider, or credit card issuer, the credited amount will appear in your account within 5 to 7 business days.
